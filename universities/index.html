GURJAS / UNIVERSITY & RESEARCH PARTNERSHIPS

Research infrastructure
for institutions that take evidence seriously.

Gurjas works with universities, research offices, faculty groups and international research teams on the problems between research ambition and research execution: methods, publication, evidence, AI workflows, integrity and impact.

PARTNERSHIP MODULES

Bring us the research problem. We build around it.

Partnerships can begin as a focused diagnostic or grow into a repeatable research-support layer for a school, department, research office or programme.

01 / RESEARCH QUALITY

Research Methods & Design

Faculty and doctoral support around research questions, conceptual models, measurement, sampling, SEM, mixed methods, qualitative design and analytical planning.

02 / PUBLICATION

Publication & Journal Intelligence

Manuscript diagnostics, journal fit, submission readiness, reviewer-response workflows and evidence-led publication strategy.

03 / AI

AI in Research Governance

Practical policies and workflows for responsible AI use, disclosure, authorship, citation verification, research integrity and faculty adoption.

04 / EVIDENCE

Research Office Intelligence

Evidence architecture, research-quality dashboards, publication intelligence, capability mapping and traceable institutional reporting.

05 / IMPACT

Programme & Impact Evaluation

Theory of change, indicator systems, evaluation design, mixed-method evidence and decision-ready reporting for university and community programmes.

06 / CAPACITY

Research Training & Clinics

Hands-on workshops and clinics for faculty, doctoral researchers, research staff and partner organisations, built around real research problems rather than generic lectures.
