Journal assessment checklist

A compact worksheet for recording what was verified, where it was verified and what remains uncertain before submission or APC approval.

Version
1.0
Published
4 August 2026
Licence
Free for non-commercial research and teaching use with attribution

Use this sequence

  1. Identity: record title, ISSN, publisher, domain, country and title history.
  2. Provenance: verify indexing and DOI records at the source, not through screenshots.
  3. Editorial conduct: inspect peer-review claims, editorial board traceability, corrections and retractions.
  4. Commercial transparency: record APCs, waivers, refund rules and payment timing.
  5. Decision: classify the evidence as verified, unresolved or contradictory; never reduce the decision to one badge.

Printable checklist

This checklist supports documented judgement; it does not certify a journal or replace institutional policy.

Intended users

Researchers, supervisors, librarians, research offices and IQAC teams.
