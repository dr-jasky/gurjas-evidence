Research question canvas

A one-page planning aid for testing whether the question, evidence and intended claim actually align before data collection begins.

Version
1.0
Published
4 August 2026
Licence
Free for non-commercial research and teaching use with attribution

1. Decision

What decision, explanation or action will this research inform?

2. Unit of analysis

Who or what is being observed, compared or explained?

3. Population and setting

For whom, where and during what period should the claim hold?

4. Constructs

Which concepts require measurement, and how will each be operationalised?

5. Comparison

What contrast, counterfactual, sequence or variation makes inference possible?

6. Evidence

What data can be accessed ethically, accurately and within the available time?

7. Claim boundary

What can the design support—and what must it not claim?

8. Failure test

What result, bias or missing evidence would weaken the intended conclusion?

Complete the canvas before choosing software, statistical tests or sample-size rules. Method follows the question and claim.
