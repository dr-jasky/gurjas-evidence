Responsible recovery

A defensible analysis begins before the software

The first task is not to generate new tables. It is to establish whether the research question, instrument, data and retained files can support a credible analytical route.

  1. Align the research logic

    Check the objectives, hypotheses, constructs and conceptual framework before choosing a method.

  2. Establish data provenance

    Inspect the questionnaire, sampling process, response evidence, coding, missingness and transformations.

  3. Select methods from the design

    Use analysis that follows from the research problem—not whichever software output is easiest to produce.

  4. Retain a reproducible record

    Ensure tables, figures, interpretations and decisions can be recreated from documented files.

Some work cannot be “fixed” through editing.

Where the instrument is invalid, the data cannot be verified or the evidence cannot support the proposed claims, the responsible next step may be a pilot, genuine recollection, redesign or narrower research question. Gurjas does not manufacture responses, conceal data problems, ghostwrite a thesis or guarantee supervisor, viva or degree outcomes.

Recurring pattern 02 · Founder-attested

A familiar title is not a verified journal identity

Researchers and faculty members have sought advice after paying intermediaries for routes represented as ABDC-ranked or Scopus-indexed. Some described payments of approximately ₹50,000–₹60,000.

i

Core verification principle: rankings and database coverage attach to an exact serial identity—title, ISSN, publisher, official domain and coverage period—not to a title string, polished website or Google result.

Step 01

Verify the source

Resolve the exact title, ISSN or ISSN-L, publisher, official domain, submission system and current database record.

Step 02

Inspect the research

Review the original thesis or project, dataset, methods and genuine contribution before promising a paper.

Step 03

Assess journal fit

Compare aims, recent articles, methodological expectations, reporting requirements and contribution threshold.

Step 04

Prepare without guarantees

Develop the manuscript for a genuine target while leaving editorial and peer-review decisions independent.

How experience becomes practice

Safeguards built into Gurjas

The value of prior experience is not the volume of work completed. It is the ability to recognise when evidence is defensible, when redesign is required and when an engagement must be declined.

What Gurjas requires

  • Original research materials and a clear account of data provenance
  • A defined research problem before methods or journals are selected
  • Methods that match the design, evidence and intended claims
  • Official-source verification for journal, metric and policy claims
  • Author understanding, accountability and control of the final work

What Gurjas refuses

  • Fabricated, purchased or knowingly misrepresented datasets
  • Ghostwritten theses or concealed authorship
  • Guaranteed publication, ranking or institutional acceptance
  • Unofficial payment routes presented as journal access
  • Claims that cannot be supported by retained evidence
