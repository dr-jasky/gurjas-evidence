A disciplined first step

Bring the decision, the evidence gap and the deadline.

Gurjas begins with a bounded diagnostic—not a sales promise. We clarify the decision at stake, inspect what evidence already exists and identify the smallest defensible next step.

  1. 01

    Frame the decision

    State the institutional, policy or research question and who must be able to rely on the answer.

  2. 02

    Map the evidence

    Separate available records, missing evidence, assumptions and constraints before recommending methods.

  3. 03

    Define a defensible scope

    Receive a clear route forward, including boundaries, outputs, ownership and the evidence standard required.

Useful first message

Include the decision, current evidence, deadline and the people who will review the work.
