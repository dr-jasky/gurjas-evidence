Content governance

Dates that mean something.

Published records the first public release. Reviewed records a substantive editorial or evidence check. Updated appears only when the content itself changes materially.

Older publication dates will not be cosmetically refreshed. Time-sensitive guidance will instead show its review status and source dates clearly.
