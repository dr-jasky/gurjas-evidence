Content governance

Dates and versions that mean something.

Published records first release. Reviewed records a substantive evidence and editorial check. Updated appears only after a material change. Version numbers change when the entry's reasoning, evidence or workflow changes—not for cosmetic edits.

No entry is described as exhaustive, and no source link is treated as a guarantee of outcome, acceptance, indexing or institutional eligibility.
