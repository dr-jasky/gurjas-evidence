Research Tools

Transparent research tools for consequential decisions

Free to use, no sign-up required, with named sources and limitations on every result. Six tools process inputs entirely in your browser. The journal shortlist, Journal Evidence Checker, Recorded APC Lookup and Reference & DOI Integrity Checker send the search term, ISSN, DOI or reference text to the named public databases only when you press their action button; APC Invoice Triage works entirely in your browser except for one optional recorded-APC lookup. Gurjas does not receive any of these queries.
