# Source review — Indian Research Grant Reference Register

**Editorial review:** 15 October 2026 (IST)  
**Scope:** Primary-source cross-check of two dated ICSSR submissions and verification of ANRF scheme-directory presence.  
**Change control:** See GitHub issue #125. This is not an availability feed, a comprehensive scheme review or an official invitation to apply.

## Verified deadline records

1. **ICSSR–NSTC (Taiwan) Joint Research Call 2026.** Official ICSSR call lists application dates 1 June to **3 August 2026**. On 15 October this advertised 2026 submission deadline had passed. Source: https://icssr.org/announcements/icssr-nstc-taiwan-joint-call-research-proposals-2026
2. **ICSSR Major and Minor Research Projects 2026–27.** The official ICSSR project call was issued on 5 August 2026, with a subsequent extension confirming **18 September 2026 at 18:00**. That advertised submission deadline had also passed on 15 October. Official primary notices:
   - https://icssr.org/announcements/call-project-proposals-under-scheme-major-and-minor-research-projects-2026-27
   - https://icssr.org/index.php/announcements/extension-last-date-submission-major-and-minor-research-project-proposals

## NOT independently verified as currently open

- **Ramanujan Fellowship**, **ANRF ARG/MATRICS**, **ANRF Inclusivity Research Grant**, **ANRF Core Research Grant**: the official ANRF portal lists scheme links, but neither a current application window nor a specific extension was established during this editorial check. Therefore all open-status claims have been removed, and the official portal is linked for re-checking: https://anrfonline.in/ANRF/HomePage
- **ICSSR–JSPS** and **ICSSR postdoctoral fellowships**: listed as official-source pointers. The review does not establish that applicants may currently submit.

## Public wording rules

- Do not describe the page as an automatically updated real-time availability tool.
- Do not show an `Open` badge without a current official submission window and a verified source/date.
- Never extrapolate next year's call from a previous year's timing.
- Clearly separate an advertised historical deadline from current application availability.
- Update a review date only after an actual source review; stale status must block publication claims, not be papered over.
- A publication date or nominal rolling scheme description does not itself establish that a call is accepting applications.
- Retain the authoritative official portal links, explicit selection scope and dated verification limitations.

## Follow-up editorial work

The two verified ICSSR deadlines are historical facts, not forecasts. For any future refresh, independently re-check every other scheme's status against the latest official funder announcement, log the source and time, and upgrade a row to 'Open' only where an official active application deadline can be corroborated.
