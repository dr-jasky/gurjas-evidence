# GJ-014 — Research Library operating contract

## Product objective

Turn the existing `/knowledge/` foundation into a governed, daily-use Research Library without creating a duplicate `/library/` route or mass-producing thin pages.

## Launch corpus

The first release contains six flagship entries split evenly across the two active pillars:

### Research integrity

1. DOI record verification
2. Journal indexing verification
3. Publication correction, retraction and update status

### Research design

4. Reporting-guideline boundary
5. Reliability–validity distinction
6. Model-specific SEM sample-size planning

## Required entry anatomy

Every public entry must contain:

- one stable canonical route;
- one direct answer near the opening;
- a practical decision workflow;
- an explicit evidence boundary;
- at least one worked use case;
- at least two official or primary sources;
- visible published and reviewed dates;
- a semantic version;
- a related tool, resource or method route;
- copyable citation text;
- a printable layout;
- conservative Article structured data.

## Governance rules

- Resolution, indexing or metadata lookup must not be presented as a universal quality verdict.
- Absence from one registry must not be described as proof of falsity.
- Reporting guidance must not be described as a substitute for design validity.
- Reliability statistics must not be described as complete validity evidence.
- SEM sample size must not be reduced to one universal threshold.
- Web-source retrieval dates must be visible.
- DOI-linked primary research may be cited through its persistent DOI record.
- Entries must not contain guarantees, black-box scores or unsupported certainty.
- Planned pillars remain roadmap cards until substantive content exists.

## Versioning

- `published`: first public release.
- `reviewed`: latest substantive evidence and editorial check.
- `updated`: latest material change to reasoning, evidence or workflow.
- patch-level cosmetic changes do not reset public dates or version numbers.

## Search boundary

Search is intentionally withheld at six entries. Launch it only after the corpus is large enough that browsing by pillar and task no longer provides a clearer route.

## Release gates

- static build and valid HTML;
- unique metadata and canonicals;
- complete sitemap and internal-link coverage;
- zero orphaned library entries;
- exact registry-to-route parity;
- direct answer, sources, boundary, citation and print controls on every entry;
- zero serious or critical Axe violations;
- zero horizontal overflow in responsive review;
- reviewed visual change before merge.

## Rollback

Revert the GJ-014 commits. No migration, user state, account, payment or stored data is introduced.
