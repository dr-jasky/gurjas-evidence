## Draft C — South Asian University SAEIC scoping enquiry

**To:** saeic@sau.int  
**Subject:** Scoping conversation — 90-day South Asia evidence-methods pilot

Dear Director and SAEIC Team,

I am writing on behalf of **Gurjas Evidence and Policy Analytics**, an independent India-based practice focused on research integrity, evidence systems, evaluation methods and transparent research tools.

SAEIC's official page describes project, training and institutional partnership routes. We would value a 30-minute scoping conversation to determine whether a small, bounded methods pilot is relevant to SAEIC's current work.

The draft concept is the **South Asia Evidence & Inclusion Lab — Founding Pilot**, designed for India and at least three additional South Asian country perspectives over 90 days. Proposed outputs are:

- a common 25-item evidence-quality rubric;
- one cross-country methods clinic;
- an approved public repository of templates and illustrative material;
- a concise comparative brief with explicit limitations; and
- a factual completion record for each participating contribution.

The pilot would not use sensitive personal data, rank institutions, issue accreditation or imply SAARC, SAU or SAEIC endorsement. Any role for SAEIC would be described only after mutual written agreement on scope, responsibilities, data, attribution and publication.

Would SAEIC be open to an initial scoping call? Subject to your availability, we can provide three possible time windows and a two-page concept note.
