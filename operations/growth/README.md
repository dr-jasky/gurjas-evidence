This directory converts the approved growth plan into controlled repository records. It is intentionally operational rather than promotional. No file here creates a membership, partnership, endorsement, client relationship, tender eligibility or editorial status.

## Operating doctrine

1. **Verify, qualify, contribute, document, then claim.**
2. Keep five evidence classes separate:
   - `FOUNDER` — a named person's scholarship, employment, qualifications or service;
   - `GURJAS_DELIVERY` — work contracted to and completed by Gurjas;
   - `PARTNER` — evidence supplied or confirmed by another organisation;
   - `PILOT` — bounded experimental work with its status and limitations stated;
   - `ASPIRATION` — a proposed or desired future state, never presented as current fact.
3. Never state or imply that Gurjas is affiliated with, recognised by, endorsed by or partnered with AMDISA, South Asian University, SAEIC, SAARC, a government, a funder, a journal index or any other organisation without current written authority covering the exact wording and logo use.
4. Prefer one bounded contribution, clinic or pilot to a vague request for an endorsement or memorandum of understanding.
5. Recheck every current official, fee, deadline, address, eligibility rule and programme status against a primary source on the day it is used.
6. Do not send an outreach message, submit an application or file a bid from a repository draft. The proprietor must approve the recipient, ask, attachment and send date outside the repository first.
7. Stop a tender at the first mandatory `FAIL`. Treat a mandatory `UNKNOWN` remaining 48 hours before closing as `NO-GO`.
8. Separate consulting sales and client records from editorial acceptance, peer review, appeals and publication ethics.
9. Every action needs an owner, deadline, evidence of completion, measurable outcome and stop rule.
10. Do not store private contact details, confidential relationship notes, client material or credentials in this public repository.

## Statement labels

Use one of these labels while researching or drafting:

- `FACT` — directly supported by retained evidence.
- `SOURCE_CLAIM` — stated by a named source but not independently established by Gurjas.
- `INFERENCE` — reasoned conclusion from cited facts.
- `RECOMMENDATION` — proposed action.
- `UNKNOWN` — unresolved; cannot support a public claim or mandatory bid gate.
