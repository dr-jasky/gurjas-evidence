# Security Policy

## Supported version

Security fixes are applied to the current production branch (`main`). Older snapshots, downloaded archives, and third-party mirrors are not maintained.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do **not** disclose suspected vulnerabilities in a public issue, discussion, or pull request.

Report security concerns privately to **support@gurjas.org** with the subject line `Security report — gurjas.org`.

Include, where possible:

- the affected URL, file, or feature;
- clear reproduction steps;
- the observed and expected behaviour;
- potential impact;
- screenshots or a minimal proof of concept that does not expose personal or confidential data.

Do not include participant-level datasets, private client documents, credentials, tokens, or other sensitive information in the initial report.

## Response process

We aim to acknowledge a credible report within five working days. We will assess severity, reproduce the issue, prepare a fix, and coordinate disclosure where appropriate. Timelines may vary according to technical complexity and third-party dependencies.

## Researcher expectations

Please act in good faith and avoid:

- accessing, changing, or deleting data that is not your own;
- degrading service availability;
- automated high-volume scanning;
- social engineering;
- publishing details before a reasonable remediation period has been agreed.

This policy does not create a bug-bounty programme or promise financial compensation.
